# Marketing page reference library

This file is a set of Mobbin links to real marketing pages from eight brands, with a note on what to look at in each. The links are for looking, not copying: the product's own design system always wins over any reference here.

Measurements are from 1920px captures scaled to 1440 and are approximate.

## Patterns that recur

Nav

- Keep the bar 56 to 72px tall at 1440; floating pill navs run 48 to 56px inside a band up to 82 (Linear, Craft, Cosmos).
- Show two to seven items: consumer tools two to four (Amie, Cosmos, Craft), platforms five to seven (Stripe, Vercel, Framer, Linear).
- Put "Log in" as plain text and one filled primary CTA at the far right (all eight); Stripe and Vercel add an outlined second button.

Hero

- Set headline cap height between 1/40 and 1/19 of viewport width; the median is 1/30, about 64 to 68px (Notion, Linear, Amie).
- Centre the hero, as six of eight do; left-align only when copy is dense or the product bleeds right (Stripe, Linear).
- Keep the headline to one to three lines, two is the mode, and the subhead to one to three lines (Vercel, Notion, Framer).
- Use one CTA or two, never three; the second is always quieter: outlined, tinted or grey (Stripe, Vercel, Notion, Framer).
- Choose one button shape and keep it: full pills (Vercel, Framer, Cosmos, Craft) or rounded rectangles of 4 to 10px (Stripe, Amie, Notion).
- Place media below the headline at full width or 68 to 71% wide (Vercel, Notion, Craft, Framer); put it right only with a left-aligned headline (Linear, Stripe).
- Hold the content column to 60 to 75% of viewport (Amie 65, Notion 68, Vercel 71, Stripe 75).

Section rhythm

- Pad section tops 86 to 130px at 1440 (Notion 86, Amie 94, Cosmos 116, Framer 120, Vercel 128, Linear 130).
- Pick one ground for the whole page, light (Stripe, Amie, Vercel, Notion) or dark (Framer, Linear).
- Alternate only with a single contrasting band or footer (Vercel startups band, Craft and Cosmos footers).
- Separate sections with hairlines or grid lines rather than colour blocks (Vercel, Linear, Notion logos).

Feature presentation

- Use two to four columns, three most often (Amie, Vercel, Notion, Linear, Framer); two when each holds a chart or figure (Stripe, Linear).
- Frame screenshots in window or browser chrome (Vercel, Craft, Linear, Notion) or crop them inside a card (Stripe, Framer).
- Keep captions to two to four lines, 15 to 30 words, with an optional "Learn more" link (Vercel, Framer, Stripe, Linear).

Proof

- Put a logo strip within one screen of the hero: five to eight logos in one row, greyscale unless colour is the brand (all eight).
- Label it with a small caption such as "Trusted by teams at" (Amie, Notion, Vercel, Framer).
- Build quote cards from logo, a two to five line quote, name and role, in a three-column masonry (Vercel, Notion, Framer).
- One or two hard numbers beat many (Linear 45% and 66%, Stripe 6.7%, Vercel "20 days").

Pricing

- Show three cards (Stripe, Vercel, Linear, Framer); two (Craft) or four (Notion) are the outliers.
- Highlight the middle tier with a tag and a filled CTA while the others stay outlined (Vercel, Notion, Linear); Framer fills the free tier instead.
- Keep the billing toggle small and inside or just above the cards (Linear, Notion, Framer chips); Stripe, Vercel and Craft skip it.

Close and footer

- Repeat the primary CTA verbatim in a short close: one line, optional one-line subhead, one button (all eight).
- Give the footer three to five link columns plus a brand column; four is the mode (Stripe 3, Vercel 3, Notion 4, Framer 4, Linear 5, Craft 5).
- End with "© year Company" plus social icons, or a Privacy, Terms, DPA line (Stripe, Vercel, Notion, Craft, Cosmos, Linear).

## When to look at which

| Cue | Open |
|---|---|
| Brief says restraint or monochrome | Amie and Cosmos: one accent or none, one CTA, narrow centred column |
| Product is the hero | Vercel and Linear: screenshots in frames, hairline grids, colour only inside the product |
| Developer audience | Vercel and Linear: drafting grid, figure numbers, terse captions |
| Enterprise or fintech trust | Stripe and Notion: logo strips, stats, dense footers, two nav CTAs |
| Consumer wellness or lifestyle | Cosmos and Craft: serif headlines, photography or paper illustration, one pill |
| Colour as identity | Stripe and Framer: one gradient element, everything else neutral |
| Editorial type | Framer and Craft: the display headline is the hero, gradient or serif on one line |

## Using Mobbin from this skill

The Mobbin MCP exposes three tools: `search_sections` for page sections, `search_screens` for whole screens and `search_flows` for multi-step journeys. Query with the brand plus one section in plain language, such as "Linear pricing section with plan cards", and keep `task_intent` identical across calls. Cite the `mobbin_url`; the `image_url` expires after 30 days, so download it. Other brands fill in when the named brand lacks that section, so check `site_name` before citing. When the Mobbin tools are absent, use the descriptions here and the product's own site.
